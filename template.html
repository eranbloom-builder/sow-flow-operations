Local portfolio demo · Synthetic data · No Microsoft account needed
01 / Generate

02 / Review & route

The PM reviews the document. Amount and dependency flags determine the approval sequence.

03 / Track & notify

Approvals advance one at a time. Rejections return to the PM and restart all approvals.

    Approval rules
    Below 50,000: Contract Manager. From 50,000 through 100,000 inclusive: Contract Manager → Finance Director. Above 100,000, or any third party, additional vendor, hardware or software: Contract Manager → Finance Director → Legal Counsel → Delivery Prime.
    Amounts are evaluated in the selected currency without conversion. Production thresholds require a defined base currency. Demo SOW language requires legal review before business use. After full approval, the final document is downloaded as PDF. Internal approval is separate from vendor and customer signatures.
    Workflow concept and original Microsoft implementation: Eran Bloom. Demo code created with AI coding assistance. No former-employer data or original exported flows.
